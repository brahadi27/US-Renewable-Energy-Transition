# The Green Footprint – US Energy Transition Analysis

Analyzing U.S. electricity generation trends to evaluate renewable energy adoption and identify states progressing fastest toward a net-zero power grid.

## Project Goals

* Analyze 20+ years of electricity generation data across U.S. states
* Compare renewable generation with coal generation
* Calculate renewable energy shares over time
* Identify each state's **Pivot Year** — when renewable generation surpasses coal
* Use trend analysis and forecasting to evaluate future clean-energy growth
* Create visualizations and an interactive state-level dashboard

## Data

* **Source:** U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) Electricity Data Browser
* **Geography:** U.S. states (50 + DC)
* **Frequency:** Annual
* **Primary energy sources:** Coal, Natural Gas, Wind, and Solar
* **Additional renewable sources:** Hydroelectric, Biomass, etc.
